Open source · macOS · everything stays on your Mac

Your Mac already knows where your time went.

Hippocampus keeps it — all local, every day — and gives it back as charts, as a journal, and as a conversation you can have out loud.

Get it on GitHub v0.1.0 release notes

Never takes a screenshot. No server, no account, no dashboard.

What it keeps

Text and events, never pixels. Everything below is read from what your Mac already writes down.

Every window, every four seconds

The app in front, the window title, the tab you were on, and when you stepped away.

Writing or reading

Keys, clicks and scroll as counts — which is what tells writing from reading, without keeping a word.

Work you delegated

Time an agent spent working while you were away counts as work, not as absence.

What came out of your hands

Commits, commands, and what you asked Claude Code and Codex, joined to the time they happened in.

Calls and what was playing

The microphone or camera open for a call — named after the meeting, if you let it read your calendar — the track in the background, the screen each window was on.

What kind of work it was

Each window is sorted into code, research, writing, meetings or leisure — and left unlabelled rather than guessed.

Rhythm

Ask the heatmap a question

Hour by weekday, over a week, a month or three. Click a cell and every chart beneath it answers the obvious question — what was I actually doing on Thursdays at four?

Work

The ticket, not just the app

A Jira tab, a question to Claude Code and a commit that name the same ticket become one line, with the client behind it read from the address. Work on a branch counts for the ticket it is named after. Click a ticket for every moment that touched it, in order.

Chat

Talk to your own day

Type or speak. Claude Code answers from the local database — with a table when a table is the answer — and a live voice you can interrupt, if you want one.

Journal

A journal written like a git log

Every evening, a summary of the day lands in your Obsidian vault. A record of what happened — never a verdict on the person who did it.

What it will not do

Take a screenshot

Not one, ever. Most apps in this category record the screen and send it to a vision model; this one reads what is already text. It costs almost nothing and never lights the orange dot.

Send your day anywhere

No server, no account, no admin dashboard. There is nothing an employer could log into — impossible by construction, not by policy. Your keys stay in the macOS Keychain.

Give you a score

Nobody does eight focused hours. A short day, a scattered day and a day of meetings are facts, not failures — and when measuring stopped, it says so instead of calling the day empty.

In Portuguese, English, Spanish, French and German — the interface, the journal and the voice.
